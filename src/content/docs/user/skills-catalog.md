PAI comes with 27 built-in skills that activate automatically based on what you ask for. You do not need to memorize skill names or use special commands. Just describe what you need and PAI picks the right skill.

This catalog is organized by the kind of work you are trying to do, with example prompts for each skill so you can see how to get started.

---

## When you need to research or investigate

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Research** | Runs thorough research on any topic, from a quick lookup to an extensive multi-source investigation. Analyzes content, extracts key insights, and delivers structured findings. | "Research the current state of quantum computing" or "Do extensive research on competitor pricing strategies" |
| **OSINT** | Gathers open-source intelligence on people, companies, or organizations by pulling together publicly available information. | "Do a background check on this company before our meeting" or "What can you find out about this organization?" |
| **PrivateInvestigator** | Helps you reconnect with people or locate contact information using ethical, publicly available sources. | "Help me find my old college roommate Sarah Chen" or "I need to locate a former colleague" |
| **AnnualReports** | Collects and analyzes annual security and threat reports from across the industry, giving you a summary of trends and key findings. | "Pull together this year's major security threat reports" or "What are the top trends in the latest annual reports?" |
| **SECUpdates** | Aggregates the latest security news, breach reports, and vulnerability disclosures so you stay current without reading dozens of sources. | "What's new in security this week?" or "Any major breaches I should know about?" |

## When you need security analysis

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Recon** | Maps out the attack surface of a target, identifying exposed services, subdomains, and potential entry points for security testing. | "Do recon on example.com" or "Map the attack surface for this target" |
| **WebAssessment** | Performs web application security testing, looking for common vulnerabilities and misconfigurations. | "Run a security assessment on our staging site" or "Test this web app for vulnerabilities" |
| **PromptInjection** | Tests AI-powered applications for prompt injection vulnerabilities, helping you find and fix weaknesses before attackers do. | "Test this chatbot for prompt injection vulnerabilities" or "Run an AI security assessment on our LLM app" |

## When you need to think through a decision

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Council** | Convenes a panel of AI agents with different perspectives to debate your question, so you hear multiple viewpoints before deciding. | "I need a council to debate whether we should rewrite our backend" or "Get different perspectives on this business strategy" |
| **RedTeam** | Stress-tests your idea by having adversarial agents attack it from every angle, exposing blind spots and weaknesses. | "Red team my product launch plan" or "Find the counterarguments to this proposal" |
| **FirstPrinciples** | Breaks a problem down to its fundamental truths and rebuilds understanding from the ground up, cutting through assumptions. | "Analyze this from first principles" or "What's the root cause of this problem?" |
| **BeCreative** | Engages in extended, deep thinking to generate multiple diverse options for creative or complex problems. | "Be creative and give me options for this architecture" or "I need some deep thinking on this challenge" |
| **Evals** | Evaluates and benchmarks AI agent performance, helping you measure whether your prompts and agents are actually working well. | "Evaluate how well this agent handles edge cases" or "Benchmark this prompt against our test suite" |

## When you need to build something

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **CreateCLI** | Generates a complete command-line tool from your description, handling argument parsing, help text, and project structure. | "Create a CLI tool that converts CSV files to JSON" or "Build me a command-line app for managing todos" |
| **CreateSkill** | Builds a new PAI skill from scratch, so you can extend PAI with your own custom capabilities. | "Create a new skill for managing my blog posts" or "I want to build a custom skill" |
| **Browser** | Automates browser interactions with built-in debugging, so you can verify how web pages look and behave, take screenshots, and troubleshoot frontend issues. | "Take a screenshot of our homepage" or "Debug why this page isn't rendering correctly" |

## When you need to create content

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Art** | Creates illustrations, diagrams, flowcharts, infographics, and other visual content to support your projects and presentations. | "Create a flowchart of our deployment process" or "Make a header image for this blog post" |
| **Prompting** | Crafts optimized prompts for AI systems, helping you get better results from language models through proven prompt engineering techniques. | "Help me write a better prompt for this task" or "Optimize this prompt for more consistent output" |
| **Aphorisms** | Finds, generates, and manages memorable quotes and sayings relevant to your topic or mood. | "Give me an aphorism about perseverance" or "Find a quote about leadership" |
| **Documents** | Processes, transforms, and extracts information from documents in various formats. | "Process this document and pull out the key points" or "Summarize this PDF for me" |
| **Fabric** | Applies specialized analysis patterns to your content for tasks like extracting key insights, creating threat models, summarizing articles, and more. | "Extract the wisdom from this article" or "Summarize this with Fabric" |

## When you need data from the web

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Apify** | Scrapes social media platforms and business directories, pulling structured data from Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Google Maps, Amazon, and more. | "Scrape the latest posts from this Twitter account" or "Pull Google Maps reviews for these restaurants" |
| **BrightData** | Scrapes web pages that are difficult to access, using progressively more capable approaches until it gets the data you need. | "Scrape this URL for me" or "I need the content from this page but it's blocking requests" |

## When you need to manage your AI

| Skill | What it does for you | Try saying |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Agents** | Creates and manages custom AI agents with specific personalities, expertise areas, and voice settings for specialized tasks. | "Create a custom agent that acts as a security advisor" or "Spin up specialized agents for this project" |
| **VoiceServer** | Controls PAI's voice notification system, letting you configure how and when PAI speaks to you. | "Start the voice server" or "Change the voice notification settings" |
| **Telos** | Tracks your life goals, projects, reading lists, and priorities, helping you see the big picture and manage what matters. | "Show me my current projects and goals" or "Update my reading list with this book" |
