Imagine an AI that already knows what you're working on when you sit down. That remembers the decision you made last Tuesday, the approach that failed last month, and the goal you're racing toward. That doesn't just answer your question — it answers it *in the context of everything it knows about you*.

That's PAI.

## An agent that magnifies you

Personal AI Infrastructure (PAI) is not a chatbot. It's not a coding assistant. It's not another AI wrapper with a settings page.

PAI is an **agent** — a system that acts on your behalf with real capabilities, persistent memory, and an understanding of who you are and what you're trying to accomplish. It was created by [Daniel Miessler](https://github.com/danielmiessler) and built on the principle that **AI should magnify everyone, not just the top 1%**.

When you ask PAI for help, it doesn't just generate text. It *thinks about your problem* using a structured algorithm, brings specialised skills to bear, delegates to other agents when needed, and verifies its own work against criteria it defined before it started building. It's systematic where other AI is reactive.

## What makes PAI different

### It remembers everything

Most AI tools forget you between conversations. PAI doesn't. Your projects, preferences, past decisions, and working style persist across every session. You never re-explain context. You never start from zero. The conversation you had last month is still part of your AI's understanding today.

### It learns from you

Rate a response 1-10 and PAI captures that signal. Express frustration or excitement naturally and PAI picks up on it. Over time, these signals compound into a detailed model of what works for you. The AI you use on day 90 is measurably better than day 1 — not because the model changed, but because PAI learned *you*.

### It has real capabilities

PAI ships with 27+ specialised skills that activate automatically — deep research, security analysis, content creation, visual design, browser automation, and more. You don't memorise commands or configure anything. Describe what you need and PAI brings the right expertise. Behind the scenes, it can spawn multiple agents working in parallel, compose skills together, and use CLI tools for deterministic operations.

### It knows your goals

This is where PAI fundamentally diverges from every other AI tool. PAI understands your mission, your active projects, your challenges, and your priorities. When you ask for help, your AI doesn't just answer the immediate question — it answers in the context of what you're actually trying to accomplish. Your goals aren't an afterthought. They're the starting point.

### It follows an Algorithm

At the centre of PAI is the Algorithm — a universal problem-solving loop that moves from current state to ideal state through verifiable iteration. Every non-trivial request goes through: Observe, Think, Plan, Build, Execute, Verify, Learn. This isn't theatre — it's how PAI ensures it builds the right thing, proves it works, and learns from every attempt.

## Who PAI is for

PAI is designed to be accessible to everyone, not just developers.

- **Anyone** who wants an AI that actually knows them and gets better over time
- **Small business owners** who want AI handling invoicing, scheduling, and customer follow-ups
- **Creatives** who want to find opportunities, create content, and manage their work
- **Developers** who want persistent memory, custom workflows, and agentic capabilities
- **Teams** building shared AI infrastructure with consistent capabilities

Whether you're an individual or an organisation, PAI is a system for understanding and realising your goals.

## Built on Claude Code

PAI is built natively on [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code). It doesn't replace Claude Code — it's the layer on top that makes Claude Code *yours*. Memory, skills, goal context, self-improvement, and the Algorithm all run on top of what Claude Code already provides. Claude Code is the engine. PAI is everything that makes it your car.

## History

PAI is created by [Daniel Miessler](https://github.com/danielmiessler), who also created [Fabric](https://github.com/danielmiessler/fabric) — a widely-used collection of AI prompts. Fabric focuses on *what* to ask AI. PAI is the evolution of that thinking into infrastructure for *how* your AI operates.
