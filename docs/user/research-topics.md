PAI's research capabilities go beyond a web search. It gathers information from multiple sources in parallel, cross-references findings, and delivers structured analysis. This guide shows you how to control the depth and get exactly what you need.

## Quick overview

When you need a fast summary — a few paragraphs, under a minute.

```
Give me a quick overview of [topic]
```

**Examples:**
- "Give me a quick overview of WebAssembly"
- "Brief summary of the EU AI Act"
- "What is retrieval-augmented generation, in simple terms?"

Words like "quick," "brief," "overview," and "summary" signal a light touch.

## Standard research

When you need a proper investigation — multiple sources, structured findings, a few minutes.

```
Research [topic]
```

**Examples:**
- "Research the current state of AI regulation in Europe"
- "Investigate the pros and cons of serverless architecture for our use case"
- "Research competitors in the developer tools space"

This is the default depth. PAI gathers from multiple sources, organises findings, and presents a structured analysis.

## Deep investigation

When you need thorough, multi-source analysis — cross-referenced findings, expert opinions, may take several minutes.

```
Do a deep investigation into [topic]
```

**Examples:**
- "Do a deep investigation into quantum computing's impact on cryptography, including recent papers and expert opinions"
- "Extensively research the market opportunity for AI-powered legal tools"
- "Thorough analysis of [company] — funding, leadership, recent news, market position, competitive landscape"

Words like "deep," "thorough," "extensive," and "investigate" signal that you want PAI to go further.

## Shaping the output

### Ask for a specific format

```
Research [topic] and give me the results as a table
Research [topic] and structure it as bullet points
Research [topic] and write it up as a one-page brief
```

### Focus on specific angles

```
Research [topic], focusing on risks and downsides
Research [topic] from a European regulatory perspective
Research [topic] with emphasis on recent developments in 2026
```

### Compare options

```
Research [option A] vs [option B] — give me a structured comparison
Compare the top 3 approaches to [problem] with pros, cons, and recommendations
```

## Tips

- **Be specific about scope.** "Research AI" is too broad. "Research AI applications in supply chain logistics" gives PAI something to focus on.
- **State your context.** "Research Kubernetes — I'm deciding whether to migrate our infrastructure" gets better results than "Research Kubernetes" alone, because PAI knows what angle matters.
- **Follow up to go deeper.** After initial results, ask "Go deeper on the second section" or "What are the counterarguments to this?"
